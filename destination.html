Panambur Beach

Panambur Beach is a popular and well-maintained beach in Mangaluru, Karnataka, known for its golden sand, clear waters, and amenities like water sports, food stalls, and life guards. It is
situated near the New Mangalore Port and< is easily accessible from the city center. The beach also
hosts special events, such as the International Kite Festival and is a great spot for watching the sunset and even dolphins.

Shree Sharavu Mahaganapathi Temple

The temple is dedicated to Lord Shiva and Lord Ganesha. The name 'Sharavu' originates from the word 'Shara' meaning arrow, tied to a fascinating legend. It holds immense religious
importance in the region and is known for its serene atmosphere that attracts devotees.

Our Lady of Rosary Cathedral, Mangalore

Our Lady of the Rosary Cathedral, also known as Rosario Cathedral, is a historic Roman Catholic cathedral in Mangalore and one of the oldest churches in Karnataka. It was originally
built by the Portuguese in 1568 and was declared a cathedral in 1851. The current structure was erected in 1910.

Ullal Beach

Ullal Beach is a serene coastal destination in Ullal, about 12 km south of Mangalore, Karnataka, known for its picturesque sandy shores, coconut trees, and views of the Arabian Sea.
It offers a tranquil atmosphere for relaxation and is near historical sites like the ruined Abbakka
Devi fort. While popular for its sunsets, the strong currents make it unsafe for swimming, though water sports are available.
